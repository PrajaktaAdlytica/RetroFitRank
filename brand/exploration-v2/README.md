# RetrofitRank — architectural symbol studies

Concept exploration generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Raster presentation only; not production vector artwork. Website branding has not been changed.

Creative brief: move beyond the rejected R monogram. Explore three original architectural symbols in cobalt #003CFF, warm ivory #FFFEF4, midnight navy #111344 and small yellow #FFDC00 accents. Use mid-century architectural-studio geometry, strong negative space, editorial serif wordmarks, light and reversed applications.

1. The Reveal: an opened building section with a detached roof and exposed inner plane.
2. The Survey: an incomplete circular survey ring surrounding angular building planes.
3. The Open Plan: interlocking courtyard walls with one yellow module pulled outward.

Recommended next direction: The Open Plan. Its plan-view geometry complements the detailed architectural illustrations and can animate by assembling its modules. Refine in vector and test at 16/32 px before production use.

Avoid: letter monograms, green, leaves, checkmarks, generic bars or cubes, swooshes, AI stars, gradients, clipart buildings and detailed skylines.
