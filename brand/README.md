# RetrofitRank — The Portfolio

Approved building-led identity: a sawtooth industrial mill, a taller mid-century office and a smaller commercial building share a baseline. One yellow window marks the focus of review.

## Files
- Horizontal logos: light, dark, cobalt, navy and white, in SVG and transparent PNG.
- Stacked logos: the same five variants in SVG and PNG.
- Standalone symbols: accent, cobalt, navy, reverse and dark.
- Optical favicon: SVG, 16/32/48/64 px PNG, multi-resolution ICO.
- Apple touch icon: 180 px. App icons: 192 and 512 px. SVG originals included.
- Identity preview board: SVG and PNG.

## Usage
Use logo-light on ivory/white; logo-dark on navy/cobalt. Use monochrome when a single ink is required. Wordmarks are outlined paths and require no font installation. Keep at least one window-height of clear space around the mark. Use the optical favicon at 16–48 px rather than shrinking the detailed symbol. Keep the regular standalone symbol at least 48 px wide; horizontal logos at least 180 px wide. Do not distort, recolour, add shadows or fill the window voids.

Palette: cobalt #003CFF, ivory #FFFEF4, navy #111344, yellow #FFDC00. Georgia serif wordmark converted to vector outlines. Artwork is a manually constructed SVG refinement of the approved third building concept.

## Website
The shared Brand component supplies all navigation and footer logos. Icons are declared in index.html and site.webmanifest. Legacy brand-mark/favicon URLs have been refreshed with this identity. The earlier R proposal is superseded.
